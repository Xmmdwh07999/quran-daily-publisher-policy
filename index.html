Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Quran Daily Publisher is a private publishing automation used by the account owner to upload and manage Quran-related content on the owner's YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram accounts.

Information accessed

  • YouTube channel identity, video metadata, and permission to upload and manage videos.
  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional-account identifiers, publishing permissions, and content-related insights needed to operate the automation.

How information is used

Access is used only to authenticate the owner's accounts, publish content explicitly configured by the owner, verify publication status, and maintain the automation. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. Account passwords are not stored by this application. Access is limited to the permissions required for publishing and account verification.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Authorization tokens are retained only while the owner keeps the automation active. The owner may revoke Google access from Google Account security settings and Meta access from Facebook/Instagram business settings at any time. To request deletion of application data, contact ebay079x@gmail.com. Requests will be handled within 30 days.

Terms of Use

This application is provided for the account owner's authorized publishing workflow. It must not be used to upload content without the necessary rights or to access accounts without permission. The service may be changed or discontinued by the owner at any time.
